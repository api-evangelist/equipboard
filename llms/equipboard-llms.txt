# Equipboard

> Equipboard is a community-sourced music-gear discovery platform. Musicians, producers, and DJs browse the equipment their favorite artists play — guitars, pedals, synthesizers, DAWs, microphones, drum machines, and more — build and share their own gear lists, read and write reviews, and follow buy-links to retailers. Equipboard does not currently publish a public developer API; this file is generated by the API Evangelist network from the public company profile.
